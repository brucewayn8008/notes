Saranik AI Agency: AI Marketing Team & Client Acquisition Plan

## Introduction
This plan outlines a comprehensive strategy for building out a full Hermes Agent-powered marketing team for Saranik AI Agency, encompassing content creation, lead generation, engagement, and performance monitoring. It also includes steps for troubleshooting existing X (Twitter) and WhatsApp agents and strategies for client acquisition.

Saranik AI Agency specializes in AI Agents & Automation, Web & Mobile App Development, and Cybersecurity VAPT for Indian businesses. Your core competitive advantage is "We run this on our own business," focusing on AI agents that win customers, with a flagship WhatsApp AI Agent offering.

---

### Phase 1: Foundational Strategy & Core Messaging (The "Why" and "What")

Before deploying more agents, a clear marketing strategy is paramount. AI agents execute, but a solid strategy guides them.

1.  **Refine Target Audience & ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):**
    *   **Specific Segments:** Beyond "Indian businesses," narrow down your niche. Consider specific industries (e.g., e-commerce, manufacturing, real estate, healthcare, education), business sizes (SMBs, mid-market, startups), or growth stages.
    *   **Pain Points:** Identify the *exact, measurable problems* your ICPs face that your services solve.
        *   *Examples:* "Missing sales opportunities due to slow customer response times on WhatsApp," "Inefficient manual lead qualification consuming sales team hours," "Risk of data breaches due to insecure web applications," "Struggling to scale customer support without increasing headcount."
    *   **Decision Makers:** Clearly define who you need to reach within these companies. (e.g., Founder/CEO, Head of Sales, CTO, Marketing Manager, Head of Operations).

2.  **Crystal-Clear Value Proposition for Each Service:**
    *   **WhatsApp AI Agent:** What is the tangible, quantifiable ROI?
        *   *Example:* "Achieve a 30-50% increase in qualified lead bookings within 30-60 days by automating 24/7 customer engagement and lead qualification on WhatsApp, freeing your sales team for high-value closures."
    *   **Web & Mobile App Development:** How does this integrate with your AI offerings and solve specific business pain points?
        *   *Example:* "Build seamless, scalable web and mobile platforms that integrate with your AI agents, transforming customer journeys and operational efficiency."
    *   **Cybersecurity VAPT:** Differentiate your security offering and connect it to overall business health.
        *   *Example:* "Proactively identify and remediate critical security vulnerabilities in your web, mobile, and API infrastructure, safeguarding sensitive data and ensuring regulatory compliance."
    *   **Overall Agency Value (Holistic Pitch):** How do your services collectively deliver unique value?
        *   *Example:* "Saranik AI Agency empowers Indian businesses with end-to-end digital solutions: intelligent AI agents to drive growth, robust web/mobile platforms for seamless operations, and rigorous cybersecurity to protect your assets. We build, automate, and secure your path to predictable success."

3.  **Content Pillars & Core Narratives:**
    *   Define 3-5 overarching themes that consistently address ICP pain points and highlight your solutions across all marketing channels.
    *   *Examples:*
        1.  **AI for Sales & Growth:** Focus on lead gen, qualification, sales automation, and customer engagement.
        2.  **Operational Efficiency & Automation:** Highlight how AI agents and automations free up human time and streamline workflows.
        3.  **Secure Digital Transformation:** Emphasize VAPT and secure development practices in the AI era.
        4.  **Building the Future (Web/App Dev):** Show how custom development underpins AI solutions and business innovation.

---

Design a specialized fleet of Hermes Agents, each with a defined role, working collaboratively under an orchestrator. Each specialist agent should use its own Hermes profile for isolated memory and skills.

1.  **Orchestrator Agent (Your "AI Head of Marketing"):**
    *   **Role:** Oversees the entire marketing workflow. Delegates tasks to specialist agents, synthesizes outputs, monitors overall campaign performance, and ensures alignment with strategic goals.
    *   **Key Tasks:** Strategy alignment, content calendar management, cross-channel coordination, performance reporting, budget monitoring (if applicable).
    *   **Setup:** Dedicated Hermes profile (e.g., `HERMES_PROFILE=marketing-orchestrator`).

2.  **Specialist Agents:**

        *   **Role:** Creates high-quality, on-brand content tailored for various platforms.
        *   **Key Tasks:**

            *   **Drafting:** Generating initial content drafts for X posts, LinkedIn articles, blog posts, email newsletters, video scripts for Reels.

            *   **Image Prompt Generation:** Creating detailed prompts for AI image generation tools (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E) to produce compelling visuals.

        *   **Role:** Qualifies inbound leads, provides necessary information, answers FAQs, and systematically nurtures leads towards a meeting booking.

            *   **Instant Inbound Response:** Providing immediate, relevant replies to inquiries on WhatsApp (your current agent) and potentially expanding to email.

    *   **D. Data & Performance Monitoring Agent:**
        *   **Role:** Systematically collects, analyzes, and reports on marketing campaign and agent performance metrics.
        *   **Key Tasks:**
            *   **Metric Collection:** Automatically gathering data on social media engagement (likes, shares, comments), website traffic (via analytics APIs), lead conversion rates at each funnel stage, meeting booking rates, and agent-specific efficiency metrics.
            *   **Reporting:** Generating structured daily, weekly, and monthly reports on key marketing performance indicators (KPIs) in easily digestible formats (e.g., markdown tables, CSV).
            *   **Anomaly Detection:** Alerting you to significant, unexpected drops or spikes in performance metrics.
            *   **A/B Testing Analysis Support:** Providing data analysis for different content strategies, messaging variations, or CTA performance.

        *   Are you actively targeting the right individuals/companies on X through strategic engagement, replies, and mentions?

    *   **Clear & Low-Friction Calls to Action (CTAs):**
        *   *Examples:* "DM us to explore an AI agent for your sales team," "Visit saranik.in/vapt for a quick security posture assessment," "Book a 15-min discovery call: [link]".
    *   **Engagement & Interaction Loop:** Configure the X agent to monitor replies and DMs, draft responses, and alert you for critical human intervention.

    *   **Diversify Lead Sources:** Don't rely solely on automated X or WhatsApp. Supplement with:
        *   **Targeted LinkedIn Outreach:** Personalized connection requests and messages to decision-makers identified through LinkedIn Sales Navigator or similar tools.

# Saranik market research: WhatsApp groups (2026-10-11)

**Question from the founder:** We sell web dev, app dev and AI agents but can't get clients. What else can we sell to these people, and how do we get paying clients through WhatsApp?

**Short answer:** The groups we monitor are mostly full of sellers, not buyers. Requests for what we sell today (websites, apps, software, AI agents) are about 0.2% of posts, and website sellers outnumber website buyers 24 to 1. Most of the agent's effort went to DMing those sellers. The meetings it booked were mostly with sellers, and several of those calls never happened on our side. The money is in (a) things people *do* ask for, like AI and short-form video, creator sourcing and WhatsApp broadcast/automation, and (b) selling our group-monitoring agent itself to the people who live in these groups. The best fit there is real-estate brokers, and we already have a working agent for them.

### 4.1 The numbers (group leads, Saranik)

|---|---|

| **DMed** | **188** |
| …of which **sellers / self-promoters** | **139 (74%)** |
| …of which **actual buyers of a service** | **18 (10%)** |
| …hiring / equity-only / co-founder posts | 16 |
| …other (property, sponsor requests, chat) | 15 |

The reply rate isn't the problem. **The targeting and the follow-through are.**

### 4.2 Why it doesn't convert, with real exchanges

**1. We are talking to the wrong people.** Sellers reply because they want to sell to *us*, or they want something free.
- After a booked "demo": "Currently we don't have any investment" … "Okah let's do this if it's for no investment!!"
- "Is it for free" / "Free me hai na?" / "Nahi mai sirf free ka saman leta hu 😡" ("No, I only take free stuff")
- One confirmed-meeting lead spent the whole thread re-posting "I'm an graphic designer… Anybody who are interested pls dm me", then quoted *us* his rates: "My charges for ai video per video under 1 min 2000 inr".
- With a freelancer, the agent pretended Saranik was the buyer: "we're looking for help with short-form video editing… on upcoming client projects". It booked that as a "confirmed meeting".

**2. Calls get booked, then we don't show up.** At least 5 of the 15 confirmed meetings have the prospect chasing us:
- "Bhai aap time dete hai call ka us time PE aap available nahi hotte" ("You give a call time but are never available at that time")
- "Hey gaurav can you join again" … two days later: "Join the meeting"
- "I didn't get any invite"
- Chrome-extension founder: "Hello?" … "Nahi toh mai kise or ko hire kar lungi" ("Otherwise I'll hire someone else") … "Pdf is not opening"
- AI-avatar video buyer: the agent wrote "Calling you right now on WhatsApp." The human wrote "Sir im calling you in 10 Minutes / In metro rightnow", then clips arrived an hour later. Nothing after that.

**3. We never give a price.** The price was asked at least 7 times and the agent dodged every time:
- "What's the cost for setup" → "It depends on your setup and target groups… on a quick Google Meet."
- "Can u let me know approx pricing" → "It is customized…"
- A broker: "is this any paid version or subscription kinda to work with you?" → "Yes, it is a subscription tailored to your setup…". No number was given and there was no follow-up.
- The only real quote went out by hand: ₹30,000 per month for a landing page plus SEO. It went to a founder who had said "We don't need website… We only need users… That's why I approach you for LinkedIn outreach." **We pitched what we sell, not what she asked for.**

**4. We promise assets we never send.** Buyers asked for proof and didn't get it:
- E-commerce buyer, asking 4 times: "I'd like to see some of your actual e-commerce projects before the meeting" → "sure… i'll send some links over now" → nothing → "when?" → eventually: "this are very basics".
- "Answer the questions I have asked in the group please" (he had asked for experience, price and past clients) → "I have compiled our past US service campaign results…", which were never sent.
- Videographer request → "Sending you their numbers and portfolio links in your direct message now." There were no such contacts.

**5. We were slow on the best-fit lead.** 14 of 18 buyers got a first DM within 1 hour, which is good. But 3 waited more than 7 days in the draft queue.
- "Looking for a Social Media & Website Management Professional" was posted Sep 1. The first sent DM went out Sep 30. No reply.
- "Need someone who can create AI Videos for reels" was posted Aug 31 and first contacted Sep 30. Reply: "Not a requirement."

**6. Our catalogue excludes the biggest demand.** The agent config's `not_offered` list contains "AI video generation / AI video editing / reels". The lead filter's `OFF_TARGET_PATTERNS` rejects `video edit`, `ai video`, `ugc`, `influencer`, `logo`, `graphic`. Those are categories 1, 2 and 4 of the demand map. The one confirmed real buyer (HeyGen avatars) came through only because the agent improvised.

**7. Our proof isn't credible.** The config's proof points claim "captured 120+ qualified leads for us so far". The data shows 18 genuine buyer DMs and no clients. A prospect who asks "what have you built?" gets three generic `saranik.ai/work/...` portfolio links, while the brand everywhere else is `saranik.in`. **[judgment]** Check that those links load and look real.
